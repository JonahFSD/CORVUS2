Communication job
By the end, ACU pitch judges should advance Wayfinder because they understand a concrete human problem, see that a working product interaction exists, and trust that the founder distinguishes a testable human-flourishing hypothesis from proven impact.

Narrative arc
1. Invert the objective of personalization.
2. Make the problem human and specific.
3. Show the built participant-controlled input.
4. Show the correctable explanation and bounded real-world action.
5. Close on the production-alpha milestone and human outcome.

Visual system
- Custom Vercel-inspired route requested by the user.
- 16:9, black canvas, white and neutral grays, thin rules, large Arial typography as a reliable Geist-compatible fallback.
- No Vercel logo or copied component; only the high-level visual grammar.
- One product-green accent, used sparingly to connect the deck to Wayfinder.
- Story image is secondary; screenshots are the proof.
