## Attribution rules

- Cite the paper's full author team for paper-specific methods or findings.
- Say "Ostervold's verified corpus" only for patterns synthesized across several records.
- Use the dissertation for the author's own chapter progression, but do not double-count overlapping articles as replications.
- Search new primary literature before claiming the approach is still state of the art or before making a high-stakes scientific recommendation.
