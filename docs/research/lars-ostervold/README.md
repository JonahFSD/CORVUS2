## Verified substantive corpus

### 1. Electrochemical biomass upgrading: degradation of glucose to lactic acid on a copper(II) electrode

- **Citation:** Lars Ostervold, Sergio I. Perez Bakovic, Jamie Hestekin, and Lauren F. Greenlee. *RSC Advances* **11**(50), 31208–31218 (2021). [DOI `10.1039/D1RA06737K`](https://doi.org/10.1039/D1RA06737K); [PubMed/PMC record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9041372/).
- **Identity evidence:** The publisher metadata associates Ostervold with ORCID `0000-0002-3439-7902` and Penn State/University of Arkansas affiliations ([DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1039/D1RA06737K)).
- **Study summary:** The authors tested room-temperature electrochemical conversion of glucose on copper in alkaline solution. They reported a maximum lactic-acid yield of 23.3 ± 1.2%, found yield to depend on alkalinity and applied potential, and proposed involvement of solvated Cu²⁺ species. **Claim status: observed as reported by the authors; not independently reproduced here.** ([full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9041372/)).
- **Access and reuse:** The version of record is publicly readable. Crossref's publisher-deposited metadata assigns the version of record a [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/), so attributed, noncommercial reproduction and adaptation are permitted subject to that license ([Crossref DOI metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1039%2Fd1ra06737k)). The [supporting information](https://www.rsc.org/suppdata/d1/ra/d1ra06737k/d1ra06737k1.pdf) should be checked separately for notices on third-party or supplementary material.

### 2. Identifying the Local Atomic Environment of the “Cu³⁺” State in Alkaline Electrochemical Systems

- **Citation:** Lars Ostervold, Raheleh Daneshpour, Madelyn Facchinei, Bolton Tran, Maxwell Wetherington, Konstantinos Alexopoulos, Lauren Greenlee, and Michael J. Janik. *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* **15**(23), 27878–27892 (2023). [DOI `10.1021/acsami.3c01852`](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.3c01852); [Penn State research record](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/identifying-the-local-atomic-environment-of-the-cusup3sup-state-i/).
- **Identity evidence:** The ACS byline links Ostervold to ORCID `0000-0002-3439-7902` and Penn State Chemical Engineering ([publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.3c01852)).
- **Study summary:** The paper combines operando Raman spectroscopy with density-functional-theory Raman standards to constrain the local structure associated with a highly oxidized copper state under oxygen-evolution conditions. The authors report evidence consistent with CuO-derived Cu–O environments rather than a single, simple bulk “Cu³⁺” phase. **Claim status: observed/model-supported as reported by the authors; not independently reproduced here.** ([Penn State abstract and citation](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/identifying-the-local-atomic-environment-of-the-cusup3sup-state-i/)).
- **Access and reuse:** The publisher page labels the article as available to purchase and carries American Chemical Society copyright; its supporting information is freely accessible, but no Creative Commons license was found for the article. Full-text transcription should therefore not be made without permission; metadata, short quotations within applicable limits, and an original summary are the safe default ([ACS publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.3c01852)).

### 3. Cation Incorporation into Copper Oxide Lattice at Highly Oxidizing Potentials

- **Citation:** Lars Ostervold, Adam Smerigan, Matthew J. Liu, Leah R. Filardi, Fernando D. Vila, Jorge E. Perez-Aguilar, Jiyun Hong, William A. Tarpeh, Adam S. Hoffman, Lauren F. Greenlee, Ezra Lee Clark, Michael J. Janik, and Simon R. Bare. *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* **15**(40), 47025–47036 (2023). [DOI `10.1021/acsami.3c10296`](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.3c10296); [Penn State research record](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/cation-incorporation-into-copper-oxide-lattice-at-highly-oxidizin/).
- **Identity evidence:** The ACS record links Ostervold to ORCID `0000-0002-3439-7902` and Penn State Chemical Engineering ([publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.3c10296)).
- **Study summary:** Using operando X-ray absorption spectroscopy, electronic-structure modeling, and post-reaction characterization, the authors report that Ba²⁺ can enter the copper-oxide lattice at oxidizing potentials, first producing an ordered BaCuO₂ phase and then a more oxidized mixed phase. **Claim status: observed/model-supported as reported by the authors; not independently reproduced here.** ([ACS abstract](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.3c10296)).
- **Access and reuse:** ACS displays the abstract and makes supporting information free, but the article is publisher-copyrighted and no Creative Commons license was found. Full-text transcription should not be made without permission ([ACS publisher record](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.3c10296)).

### 4. A Versatile Electrochemical Cell for *Operando* XAS

- **Citation:** Lars Ostervold, Adam S. Hoffman, David Thompson, Simon R. Bare, and Ezra Lee Clark. *ChemCatChem* **16**(15), e202400072 (2024). [DOI `10.1002/cctc.202400072`](https://doi.org/10.1002/cctc.202400072); [Penn State research record](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/a-versatile-electrochemical-cell-for-operando-xas/).
- **Identity evidence:** The DOI and DOE metadata link Ostervold to ORCID `0000-0002-3439-7902` and Penn State ([DOE OSTI record](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/2407199)).
- **Study summary:** The authors describe and benchmark a configurable two-compartment electrochemical XAS cell with optional liquid and gas flow, pH and temperature monitoring, and support for multiple catalyst forms. They also use it to report that tetrabutylammonium suppresses bulk copper oxidation under their test conditions. **Claim status: observed as reported by the authors; not independently reproduced here.** ([DOE OSTI abstract](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/2407199)).
- **Access and reuse:** DOE OSTI provides a public [accepted manuscript](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2407199), while the publisher record identifies Wiley-VCH copyright. No Creative Commons license was found. Public read access does not itself grant wholesale republication rights, so use metadata and an original summary unless permission is obtained ([DOE OSTI record](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/2407199); [Penn State copyright metadata](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/a-versatile-electrochemical-cell-for-operando-xas/)).

### 5. Advanced EXAFS analysis techniques applied to the L-edges of the lanthanide oxides

- **Citation:** Adam Smerigan, Adam S. Hoffman, Lars Ostervold, Jiyun Hong, Jorge Perez-Aguillar, Ash C. Caine, Lauren Greenlee, and Simon R. Bare. *Journal of Applied Crystallography* **57**(6), 1913–1923 (2024). [DOI `10.1107/S1600576724010240`](https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576724010240); [full publisher HTML](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2024/06/00/xx5061/index.html).
- **Identity evidence:** Ostervold is listed with Penn State Chemical Engineering alongside recurring collaborators Smerigan, Hoffman, Greenlee, and Bare. The paper does not display his ORCID, so the institutional affiliation and co-author continuity are the disambiguating evidence ([IUCr byline](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2024/06/00/xx5061/index.html)).
- **Study summary:** The authors collected and modeled L-edge EXAFS spectra for the lanthanide oxides except promethium, evaluated removal of multielectron-excitation artifacts, and compared constrained lattice-expansion parameterizations. They report that a non-cubic expansion preserved fit quality and captured anisotropic expansion in Nd₂O₃ better than a cubic constraint. **Claim status: observed/model-supported as reported by the authors; not independently reproduced here.** ([IUCr article](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2024/06/00/xx5061/index.html)).
- **Access and reuse:** The version of record is open access under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which permits attributed reproduction and adaptation; the publisher explicitly states this permission ([IUCr license notice](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2024/06/00/xx5061/index.html)). A public [PDF](https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2024/06/00/xx5061/xx5061.pdf) and a [DOE OSTI copy](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/2476855) are available.
